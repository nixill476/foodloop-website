Our purpose

One platform. Six ways to support the food system.

FoodLoop helps people discover useful connections and services. Farmers and service providers remain in control of their own products and services.

🌾 Connect

Help farmers find interested buyers and relevant local services.

♻️ Reduce waste

Encourage safe food recovery, composting and circular use of by-products.

🤝 Empower

Make information and opportunities easier to discover in rural communities.

What FoodLoop supports

Our six focus areas

Practical ideas for a student project addressing real food-system challenges.

01

Farmer–Buyer Connection

Farmers can share details about their produce and connect with potential buyers directly. FoodLoop does not sell produce.

02

Local Cold Storage

Discover nearby cold-storage services to help store perishable produce and reduce post-harvest losses.

03

NGO & Village Outreach

Support links between NGOs, community groups and remote villages for food awareness and rural assistance.

04

Zero Waste & Circular Economy

Explore food rescue, composting and safe reuse of suitable by-products such as plant residues.

05

Rural Empowerment

Highlight self-help groups, rural entrepreneurs and community-led food-processing opportunities.

06

Pre-Planting Demand Matching

Farmers can share planned crops before planting and buyers can register interest to support better crop planning.
